## Context
A multi-domain operating environment depended on fragmented source systems across CRM, MIS, school systems, and HR platforms. Business teams needed more dependable data movement for analytics, reporting, and operational decision-making.

## Challenge
Data was spread across many systems, workflows were difficult to coordinate, and manual processing created delays for stakeholders. The platform needed to support 12+ business units across 5+ countries without exposing confidential operational details.

## Approach
Designed and led an enterprise data integration platform with governed ETL/ELT pipelines, API-based integration patterns, workflow orchestration, and reliability controls. The work focused on repeatable data movement, clearer operational ownership, and analytics-ready outputs.

## Impact
- 20+ enterprise systems integrated
- 150+ pipelines and workflows built
- 12+ business units supported
- 5+ countries supported
- 100+ users and stakeholders impacted
- 90% processing effort reduction
- 30 hours of manual work saved per week
- Approximately 99% data availability for critical reporting workflows
