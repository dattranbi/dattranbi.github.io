## Context
Business teams needed faster ways to summarize insights and analyze documents without removing human judgment from the review process.

## Challenge
Manual analytical workflows required too many steps and too much repetitive handling. The solution needed to reduce workflow complexity while keeping review, acceptance, and decision accountability with people.

## Approach
Designed AI-assisted automation combining Python, APIs, LLM capabilities, automation scripts, and n8n-based workflow orchestration. The workflow emphasized guided steps, repeatable processing, and human-in-the-loop review.

## Impact
- Automated insight summarization and document analysis workflows
- Supported 15+ users and teams
- Reduced workflow complexity from 10 manual steps to 2 guided steps
- Reduced task completion time from 4 hours to approximately 10 minutes
- Processed 100+ reports, documents, or requests per day
- Approximately 90% accepted outputs under human-in-the-loop review
- Improved decision speed while reducing repetitive manual work

## Capabilities Demonstrated
- AI workflow automation
- LLM-assisted analysis
- Python automation
- API integration
- n8n workflow orchestration
- Human-in-the-loop review
