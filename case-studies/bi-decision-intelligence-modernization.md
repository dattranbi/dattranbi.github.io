## Context
Reporting and decision support were spread across Sales, Marketing, Operations, HR, Academic, IT, Finance, and Materials Management domains. Leaders needed a more consistent way to interpret business performance.

## Challenge
Fragmented reports, inconsistent KPI definitions, and spreadsheet-heavy processes slowed decision cycles. The work required a shared analytics layer that could serve executive and management users while preserving confidential business details.

## Approach
Led BI modernization through standardized dashboards, semantic modeling, KPI alignment, executive reporting views, and refresh patterns that supported hourly and near real-time analysis where appropriate.

## Impact
- 20+ dashboards and analytical reports delivered
- 50+ executive and management users supported
- 27+ KPIs standardized
- 6+ data and semantic models created
- Reporting cycle improved from 5 days to 0.5 hours
- 90% manual Excel/reporting reduction
- Hourly and near real-time refresh patterns enabled
- 30+ recurring monthly users

## Capabilities Demonstrated

- BI modernization
- Semantic modeling
- KPI standardization
- Executive reporting
- Data storytelling
- Decision intelligence
